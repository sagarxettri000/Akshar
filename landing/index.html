How studying with Akshar works

One loop, repeated until a topic holds: read it, get it explained, then prove it to yourself.

  1. 1

    Choose what to study

    Pick a track (NEB Grade 11, NEB Grade 12, CEE, or IOE), then the subject, topic, and language of the notes. Every level only offers lessons that actually exist.

  2. 2

    Read, then ask

    The study notes stay on screen as the source. Ask a question in English or Nepali and Gemma 4 answers from those notes only — and says so when the notes do not cover something.

  3. 3

    Practise and check

    Generate a short set of multiple-choice questions, answer them all, then see which were right and why. Flashcards are there for a quick recall pass before an exam.

What Gemma 4 does here

Gemma 4 is not a badge on this page — it does the teaching work. Akshar calls Google's hosted model gemma-4-26b-a4b-it through the official google-genai SDK, grounded on the lesson you are reading.

  • ExplainSummarises the lesson you have open.
  • AskAnswers your question from the lesson, in English or Nepali.
  • PractiseWrites multiple-choice questions with an explanation for each answer.
  • FlashcardsBuilds prompt-and-answer cards for recall practice.
How the output is checked
  • Responses are parsed defensively — plain JSON, fenced blocks, or JSON inside a sentence.
  • Every question needs four options A–D, exactly one marked answer, and a non-empty explanation before it is shown.
  • Malformed items are dropped and logged without their content; a failed call is shown as an error, never as an answer.
  • The API key is never logged and is redacted from error messages.

What the content is — and is not

Source material and AI output are kept visibly separate, because a student needs to know which one they are reading.

Study notes — the source

Curated lesson notes written by our team covering standard NEB, CEE, and IOE topics, in English and Nepali, with formulas written out in plain text.

They are original text for this demo: not official NEB, CEE, or IOE material, and no past papers or copyrighted textbooks are copied. Source-attributed curriculum material is the next thing we are building.

Gemma 4 output — a study aid

Explanations, practice questions, and flashcards are generated from the lesson on screen and labelled as AI-generated where you read them. They are not official exam questions.

Generated questions are checked for structure, not for truth. Treat an answer as a prompt to check your understanding against the notes, not as a final authority.
