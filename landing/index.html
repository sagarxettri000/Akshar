Sources and limits

The notes are the source

Lesson notes are written by our team for standard NEB Grade 11–12, CEE, and IOE topics, in English and Nepali. They are original text for this build: not official NEB, CEE, or IOE material, and no past papers or copyrighted textbooks are copied. Source-attributed curriculum material is the next thing we are building.

Gemma 4 is the study aid

Akshar calls Google's hosted gemma-4-26b-a4b-it through the Gemini API, grounded on the lesson on screen. Explanations, questions, and flashcards are labelled as AI-generated wherever you read them, and are never presented as official exam material.

How output is checked

Responses are parsed defensively — plain JSON, fenced blocks, or JSON inside a sentence. A question needs four options A–D, exactly one marked answer, and a non-empty explanation before it is shown; malformed items are dropped. A failed call is shown as an error, never as an answer. The API key stays on the server and is redacted from messages.
