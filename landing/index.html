Understand it, then practise it.

Akshar helps Nepali students master NEB Grade 11–12 and prepare for CEE / IOE with clear Nepali/English explanations, practice questions, and flashcards — with Gemma 4 doing the real teaching work.

Launch the app View source

The problem

Nepali learners preparing for NEB and entrance exams often rely on generic answers or English-only resources that do not match their curriculum or language. Trustworthy, level-appropriate support is uneven — especially on modest devices and connections. Akshar is built to close that gap.

Built on Gemma 4

Akshar calls Google’s hosted Gemma 4 through the official google-genai SDK (gemma-4-26b-a4b-it). Every result is validated before it reaches the student: JSON is parsed defensively, and each question’s options, answer, and explanation are checked. Failures surface as honest errors, never as a fake answer.

  • Grounded summaries, multiple-choice questions, and flashcards
  • Strict output validation with clear error types
  • API keys kept in secrets and redacted from errors
